The Story

About The Forge & Fable

A hobby that got serious — and a printer that never stops running.

From the hobby room to your table

The Forge & Fable started the way most things like this do — a dungeon master who couldn't find the minis he needed and decided to print them himself. One printer became two. Two became a small operation. And somewhere along the way, other people started asking if they could order too.

We're based in Lima, OH and we print everything ourselves — no outsourcing, no dropshipping. When you order from us, a real person in a real workshop is running your file and packaging your order. We know what a bad print looks like and we don't ship them.

We specialize in TTRPG miniatures and terrain — the stuff that makes your table look like the world you're imagining. Resin for detail minis, PLA for terrain and props. We also offer over 200 coin prop designs in resin — great for loot drops, campaign rewards, or just looking cool on the shelf.

If you have an STL, we'll print it. If you don't, we can help you find one. And if you're in Lima, come pick it up — we like meeting the people who actually play these games.

The Equipment

What We Print On

RESIN PRINTER — 4K LCD
Our resin printer handles miniatures, coin props, and display pieces. 4K resolution at 0.03–0.05mm layer height means surface detail that rivals injection molding. Every resin print is washed and UV cured before it ships.
RESIN MINIS COIN PROPS DISPLAY PIECES
BAMBU LAB FDM — WITH AMS
Our Bambu Lab FDM printer with the AMS (Automatic Material System) handles terrain, scatter pieces, and large builds. The AMS lets us print up to 4 filament colors in a single job — no painting required for base terrain colors. PLA Basic and PLA Matte in 56 colors.
