COIN PROPS
Lots of ways! Most DMs use them as loot drops and quest rewards — hand a player a physical Kraken's Seal when they raid a sea temple, or a Dragon Egg when they loot a wyrm's hoard. They also make great inspiration tokens, faction favors ("you've earned the Thieves' Guild's trust — here's their mark"), or condition markers on the battle map. They're the kind of physical detail that makes a moment stick. Some folks just collect them because they look great on a shelf.
Single coins are $2.00 each, and you save when you bundle: 3 for $5, 5 for $8, or 10 for $15. Mix and match any designs across any themes — the discount applies automatically in your cart based on how many coins you add.
Round coins are printed at roughly half-dollar size (around 30mm) — big enough to show off the detail, small enough to handle and stack like real coins. The square dragon egg and fire dragon coins are a bit larger (35–40mm) since they're more ornate display pieces.
All coins are printed in resin for maximum detail. The default is grey, which is perfect if you plan to paint them. We keep limited stock of red clay and black resin as well — just pick your color in the shop, and we'll confirm availability before printing.
MATERIALS & QUALITY
Resin captures incredibly fine detail — it's what we use for miniatures, coins, and display pieces where crisp features matter. It's a little more brittle, so it's best for pieces that sit on a shelf or the table rather than getting tossed in a bag. PLA (printed on our Bambu FDM) is tougher and better for terrain, large builds, and anything that takes handling. We pick the right material for each job.
Our Bambu printer has an AMS (Automatic Material System) that can print up to 4 filament colors in a single piece — no painting required for base colors. Most print shops only do single-color terrain, so this is something we're genuinely excited to offer. Dungeon tiles, scatter terrain, and props can come ready to play right off the plate.
Right now prints come unpainted — raw resin or PLA, ready for you to prime and paint. Coins default to grey resin which takes paint beautifully, but also looks great as-is. Multicolor terrain comes with its base colors printed in. (A painting service may come down the road!)
We stand behind every print. If something fails because of our equipment or process, or arrives damaged, we'll reprint it at no charge. Just send us a photo and we'll make it right.
